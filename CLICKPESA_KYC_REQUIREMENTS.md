# ClickPesa KYC Requirements

This checklist summarises the KYC requirements ClickPesa publishes for registered businesses and organisations in Tanzania.

KYC is completed in **Merchant Dashboard → Settings → KYC**. Before approval, account usage is limited; after approval, ClickPesa removes the pre-KYC transaction and API limits and can enable the restricted payment services.

## Requirements for a limited company

### Business documents

- Detailed Information about Company from BRELA
- Certificate of Registration
- TRA TIN Certificate showing the company name
- Business Licence Certificate showing the company name
- Memorandum and Articles of Association
- Board Resolution (use the ClickPesa template available in the KYC page)
- Signed ClickPesa Customer Service Level Agreement / Terms and Conditions, with signatures, company seal, and witness where required

### Beneficial-owner and authorised-person documents

Provide identity documents for each required:

- Authorised personnel
- Director
- Shareholder

ClickPesa accepts a NIDA ID, Voter's ID, driving licence, or passport for these individuals.

### Conditional documents

- Regulatory licence — if applicable
- VAT Certificate — if applicable

## Other entity types

| Business type | Additional or entity-specific requirements |
|---|---|
| Sole proprietor | BRELA Detailed Information about Business Name; owner ID; Power of Attorney only where another person opens/operates the account. |
| Partnership | Partnership Deed; Partner Resolution; IDs for authorised personnel, directors, and partners. |
| Trust | ClickPesa Declaration Form; Trust Deed; Board Resolution; IDs for authorised personnel, directors, and trustees. |
| Cooperative | ClickPesa Declaration Form; cooperative bylaws; member list; Board Resolution; IDs for authorised personnel and directors. |
| NGO / CSO | ClickPesa Declaration Form; constitution; Board Resolution; IDs for authorised personnel, directors, and founder members. |
| VICOBA | ClickPesa Declaration Form; registration certificate; local-government introduction letter; group constitution; ClickPesa account-opening resolution; IDs for authorised personnel and group leader. |

Most entity types also require their registration certificate, TRA TIN certificate, business licence, and the signed ClickPesa agreement. The detailed list displayed in the KYC page depends on the selected business type.

## Submission and approval

1. Ensure documents are current and match the registered business name.
2. Upload the business documents and each required UBO/authorised-person ID in **Settings → KYC**.
3. Complete the ClickPesa forms/templates and any KYC-search payment if prompted.
4. ClickPesa compliance reviews the submission and sends the outcome by email. They may request additional information.

If a business is not registered, ClickPesa says it must be registered first. For assistance or a business-type change, contact `compliance@clickpesa.com` or `support@clickpesa.com`.
